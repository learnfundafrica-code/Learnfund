Frequently Asked Questions

What qualifies as an emergency?
Medical emergencies, family crises, urgent travel, academic emergencies, or any unexpected situation that threatens your education.
How fast is approval?
Existing users: 1-2 hours. New users: 4-6 hours. Emergency hotline can expedite urgent cases.
What are the repayment terms?
Repayment from 7 to 60 days, depending on amount. Flexible terms available for medical cases.
Can I request multiple emergencies?
Yes, but total outstanding amount is limited based on your credit score and repayment history.
