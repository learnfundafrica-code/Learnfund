Frequently Asked Questions

How is my credit score calculated?
Your score is based on payment history (40%), credit utilization (20%), length of credit history (20%), credit mix (10%), and new credit (10%).
Does my score affect loan approval?
Yes! Higher scores get faster approval, lower interest rates, and higher loan limits.
Can employers really see my score?
Only if you give permission. Many employers use credit history to assess responsibility and reliability.
What if I have a late payment?
Late payments can lower your score. Contact us immediately if you're having difficulty — we can arrange payment plans.
How often is my score updated?
Your score updates monthly as new payment data is recorded.
