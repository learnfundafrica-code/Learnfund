Frequently Asked Questions

How long does approval take?
Approval timing depends on how quickly the institution confirms enrollment. In the pilot, we aim for a 24–72 hour window.
Is collateral required?
No. Eligibility is based on verified enrollment at a partner institution — no assets or guarantors.
Where does the money go?
Directly to the institution. Funds are never handed to the student as cash, keeping the process clean and auditable.
What if my institution isn't part of the pilot yet?
You can still register interest. The pilot begins with UDSM and UCC, with expansion planned after review.
