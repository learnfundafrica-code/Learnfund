Proposed Pilot Network

Accepted Colleges & Universities

LearnFund starts with a focused pilot. The institutions below are currently accepted into the proposed pilot network.

Accepted

UDSM — University Computing Centre (UCC)

The proposed starting pilot environment. UCC provides a defined student community where the LearnFund concept can be tested, measured, and improved before considering wider expansion.

Focus: Students and eligible parents/guardians.
Accepted

College of Business Education (CBE)

Accepted into the proposed pilot network. CBE students and eligible parents/guardians can participate under the same verification and eligibility framework used at UCC.

Focus: Students and eligible parents/guardians.

More Institutions

Additional colleges and universities will be considered as the pilot progresses. Each new institution will be subject to the same verification and stakeholder review.

Who Can Participate?

A simple eligibility outline for the proposed pilot.

Participants

  • Students of accepted institutions
  • Eligible parents / guardians
  • Students or families with education-related needs

Basic Requirements

  • Relevant student information
  • Willingness to provide verification details
  • Clearly identified education-related need
  • Compliance with applicable institutional procedures
