Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the transport pass?
Show your QR code to the driver, they scan it, and your fare is automatically deducted. No cash needed!
What if my pass expires mid-semester?
You can renew anytime through the app. We'll send reminders before expiration.
Can I use the pass for any transport?
Currently works with boda-boda, shuttles, and buses that are part of our partner network.
What if I lose my pass?
Report immediately in the app to freeze your pass. We'll issue a replacement with remaining balance.
