Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my meal card?
Apply online, get approved within 24 hours, and receive your digital card instantly. Physical cards are delivered to your campus within 3-5 days.
Where can I use the meal card?
At all partnered vendors on your campus. We have 100+ vendors across Tanzania, with more joining every week.
What if I run out of funds?
You can top up anytime through our app. We also have emergency meal support for urgent situations.
What is emergency meal support?
If you're facing financial hardship, you can request emergency meal credits to ensure you don't go hungry while studying.
Can I use the card at non-campus vendors?
Currently, the card works at campus-affiliated vendors. We're expanding to off-campus locations soon!
