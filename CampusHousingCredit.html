Frequently Asked Questions

How much deposit can I get financed?
We cover up to 100% of your security deposit, up to TZS 500,000 depending on your credit history.
What are the repayment terms?
Repayment terms range from 30 to 180 days, with 0% interest if paid within 30 days.
Are all properties verified?
Yes! All partner properties are inspected and verified for safety, cleanliness, and fair pricing.
What if I need to move out early?
Contact us immediately. We'll work with you and the landlord to find a solution.
